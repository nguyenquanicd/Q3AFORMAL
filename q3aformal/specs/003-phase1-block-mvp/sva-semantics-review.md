# Phase 1 SystemVerilog Assertions semantics review

The integrated Windows candidate implements the approved one-clock, two-valued profile. The review uses the sampled state before the register update and synchronous design reset on the next transition. The evidence covers the property forms and finite expression limits in the published profile. The reviewed profile meets AC-02 on the integrated candidate. The evidence applies to the documented bounded grammar and engine limits. Named sequence declaration support, multi-clock execution, asynchronous assertion abort, empty sequences, and liveness remain outside the approved Phase 1 profile.
