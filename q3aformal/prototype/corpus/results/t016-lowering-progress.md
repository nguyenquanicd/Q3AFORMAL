# T016 RTL/SVA Lowering Progress

**Date:** 2026-11-15  
**Profile:** Q3A-P0-R0.9.1  
**Status:** In progress; restricted continuous-assignment, combinational-process, single-clock sequential, and temporal-property T-IR slices are implemented. The complete canonical T-IR and proof semantics are not implemented.

## Implemented preparation

The parser now retains continuous-assignment source structure instead of discarding it:

- `ContinuousAssignmentSyntax` stores lvalue and right-hand-side token spans plus the source location of `assign`.
- `ParsedModule::continuous_assignments` stores module-scope continuous assignments.
- A `GenerateSyntax::Other` node stores an optional assignment payload for generated continuous assignments, preserving its generate-tree location for later expansion.
- `ProcessSyntax` retains `always_comb`/`always_ff`, the positive-edge clock name, and a statement tree for blocks, `if`/`else`, `case`/`casez`, blocking assignments, and nonblocking assignments.
- `PropertyDeclarationSyntax` and `PropertyCheckSyntax` retain property names/check kinds, bound clocks, property-reference names, and source spans for temporal sequences. The lowerer consumes these spans for its restricted temporal-property T-IR representation; the parser does not yet build a standalone typed SVA syntax tree.

The lowerer builds typed expression trees for literals, signal/constant references, unary/binary operators, conditional expressions, concatenations, constant bit/part selects, and guarded `$past` expressions. The original lowering milestone emitted deterministic JSON labeled `Q3A-TIR-0.3`; the current schema and SVA history-guard alignment are recorded in the 2026-11-19 T-IR 0.4 increment below. That earlier output included source/profile identity, signal/constant source spans, top signal/parameter metadata, combinational equations, clock names, initial-state policy, next-state equations, temporal properties, symbolic sequence automata, and history-guard metadata. The supported procedural slices are: (1) one-clock `always_ff` with nested `if`/`else`, `case`/restricted `casez`, nonblocking whole-signal assignments, exact widths, and hold behavior on paths without an assignment; and (2) `always_comb` with blocking whole-signal assignments, nested `if`/`else`, exact `case` with constant labels/default, and `casez` with explicit-width binary patterns containing only `0`, `1`, `Z`, or `?`. In sequential branches, the final nonblocking assignment to a target wins; unmatched cases hold the target. Combinational targets must be assigned on every path. Case selector/label widths must match exactly, case items preserve source-order priority, and `casez` is represented by masked equality: `(selector & mask) == (pattern & mask)`. The lowerer also flattens continuous assignments, complete `always_comb`, and the supported one-clock `always_ff` subset recursively through parameterized or unparameterized, generate-free module trees, including multiple instances of one child module. Resolved child parameters/localparams are retained under instance-qualified names; child-local signals/state use full hierarchical names that preserve instance separation. Supported input expressions must exactly match formal widths. Direct and nested scalar child output ports may connect to constant parent-vector bit-selects, including generate-variable indices, when the complete parent bus is covered exactly once. At this earlier hierarchy-lowering milestone, generated hierarchy was unsupported. The later limited instance-only static-generate implementation and its bounds are described in the 2026-11-17 addendum below. A dependency-cycle check rejects combinational loops across lowered equations.

The limited SVA lowering accepts inline or named property checks for `assert`, `assume`, and `cover`; one positive-edge clock; one-bit sequence predicates; `##N` with constant nonnegative `N`; `|->`/`|=>`; positive exact or bounded consecutive repetition; and `$past(expr[,N])` with positive constant `N`. Each sequence emits a symbolic finite automaton with explicit start/accept states and ordered transitions carrying predicates, cycle delays, and positive repetition bounds. A property contains separate antecedent/consequent automata and an implication mode; each possible antecedent endpoint launches the consequent at that sample for `|->` or one sample later for `|=>`. `$past` nodes are lowered to generated history-data state references with shift next-state equations. A generated validity-state chain starts false, becomes valid after the required number of samples, and continues through RTL reset; nested `$past` depths accumulate. History-data initial values remain unconstrained and are ignored until the property guard is true. The prototype currently caps generated history depth at 65,536 samples as an implementation resource guard, not a product language limit. Complete event/sampling semantics, executable property monitors, and solver-ready proof obligations remain open. Compound-sequence repetition, full IEEE expression sizing, complete generated-hierarchy semantics, expression-valued output actuals, and multiple clocks remain outside this slice. Inline and named child properties are now recursively flattened through nested hierarchy with instance-qualified names, mapped clocks/ports, and namespaced history state; full event-sampling semantics and executable monitors remain open. Unsupported semantics stop with diagnostics instead of being silently omitted or approximated.

## 2026-11-19 T-IR 0.4 SVA history-guard alignment increment

The current lowerer emits `Q3A-TIR-0.4`. It retains the maximum property history depth for the generated history-state chain and adds separate antecedent/consequent warm-up delays and guard signals. Each sequence's required warm-up is computed from the earliest sample offset of each history-using term: a `$past` depth `d` first used at offset `o` requires `max(d-o, 0)` valid samples at that sequence's start. This preserves delayed-term behavior and avoids suppressing an implication because a consequent's history guard is inactive at the antecedent start.

The encoder and replay apply each operand's guard at its own sequence start for bounded assertions, assumptions, implication covers, and k-induction. The test `test_sva_delayed_past_implication.ps1` confirms immediate and delayed antecedent/consequent cases, BMC fail/cover outcomes, and independent witness replay. `test_inductive_sva_implication.ps1` now proves the supported non-overlapped guarded-history implication and validates a delayed guarded-history assumption counterexample. `test_result_bundle.ps1` confirms newly written bundles carry T-IR 0.4; `test_bundle_reopen.ps1` confirms legacy T-IR 0.3 bundles still reopen. Fresh build ID: `8179c6f3e88ed72936b761c694e84cddd9beea323bfa99fd2aab940cb1d19e7d`.

This is focused evidence for history-guard placement in the accepted finite linear SVA subset. Full IEEE sampling semantics, complete source-to-T-IR conformance, multi-clock support, unbounded operators, and proof correctness beyond current acceptance evidence remain open. T016/T017 are not complete.
