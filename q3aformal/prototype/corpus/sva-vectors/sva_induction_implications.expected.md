# Expected induction behavior — Bounded scalar sequences and implications

**Vector ID:** SVV-005  
**Profile:** Q3A-P0-R0.9.1

`--prove-inductive` accepts scalar one-bit predicates and same-sample sequences whose one-bit terms are joined by `##0`. A standalone scalar assertion sequence may also use fixed delays and positive bounded repetition ranges, provided its automaton is one linear match path, its path expansion has at most 4096 combinations, and its maximum offset plus `k` fits the 10000-transition limit. Delayed scalar assertions cannot use guarded `$past`. This is an assertion of a sequence match at every start frame: `a ##1 q` requires `a` at frame t and `q` at t+1, and a false `a` is a failure rather than an implication that vacuously succeeds. When repetition has multiple allowed lengths, any complete match path may satisfy the sequence. `1'b1[*2] ##2 1'b1[*2]` checks fixed delay and repetition; `1'b1[*1:2] ##1 q` checks variable repetition path semantics. Finite bounded delay ranges ##[m:n] are supported by existential endpoint matching; unbounded delay remains unsupported. Assertion implications (`|->` and `|=>`) may use one-bit linear sequence operands with fixed delays or finite bounded delay ranges (##[m:n]) and positive bounded repetition. Every matching antecedent path creates an obligation: the consequent starts at its endpoint for `|->` and on the following sample for `|=>`; each consequent may itself match any allowed repetition path. For example, `(a ##1 b) |-> (c ##2 d)` matches `c,d` from the sample where `b` matches. The complete horizon includes the longest antecedent path, the implication delta, and the longest consequent path. Assumptions, including implication assumptions, may use the same fixed-delay/finite-bounded-delay-range/bounded-repetition linear sequence subset. Only starts whose complete maximum horizon fits the proof window are applied; delayed assumptions with guarded `$past` are rejected. A delayed implication assertion cannot use guarded `$past`; the guard is only valid for the existing same-sample property forms. A same-sample sequence such as `a ##0 b` is interpreted as `a && b`. An implication `(a ##0 b) |-> (c ##0 d)` is encoded per frame as `(!a || !b || c) && (!a || !b || d)`. If a same-sample overlapped implication uses guarded `$past`, the property guard qualifies the complete implication as `!history_valid || implication_predicate`.

The `p_y_implies_a` property must produce checked UNSAT base and step obligations and `Q3A_INDUCTION_STATUS PROVEN`. The false `p_false_implication` property must produce a reachable base counterexample, `FAIL`, and successful independent replay. The assumption top must prove `p_assumption_consequence` under the same-cycle `a |-> b` environment assumption. `p_q_tracks_past` must prove across a one-bit state transition with the guarded `$past` consequent.

For bounded delayed implication operands, `p_delayed_overlapped_implication` and `p_delayed_nonoverlapped_implication` must prove, including the repeated antecedent's possible match endpoints. `p_false_delayed_implication` must report `FAIL` with a base witness independently replayed at property-start frame 0. The source-level `(a ##1 b) |-> (c ##2 d)` vector also remains a reachable false implication and must be replayed rather than rejected as unsupported.

In `sva-induction-delayed-assumptions.q3a`, `p_sequence_target` must prove under `a ##1 b`, and `p_implication_target` must prove under `(a ##1 b) |-> (c ##1 d)`. `p_false_target_under_delayed_assumptions` must produce a reachable `FAIL` witness at frame 0 that independently passes replay against both assumptions. The base and step encoder and replay must omit only assumption starts whose full maximum horizon crosses the corresponding proof window.

The separate `sva-induction-delayed-guarded-assumption.q3a` manifest must fail closed because `$past(a) ##1 b` combines a delayed assumption with a property-level history guard that cannot be shifted safely.

The vector and its companion range manifests include scalar, one-term implication and assumption cases, a false implication for replayed failure, a guarded `$past` invariant, same-sample `##0` sequences, one-sample `|=>` assertion/assumption cases, fixed-delay/repetition and bounded-variable-repetition standalone sequences, literal and parameter-resolved delay-range sequence/implication/assumption cases, and replayed false sequence/implication cases. Same-sample and bounded delayed assumptions are supported at complete proof-window starts; delayed assumptions with guarded `$past`, delayed implication assertions with guarded `$past`, unbounded delay ranges and other temporal forms remain outside induction. `PROVEN` applies to the serialized generated T-IR model and does not certify source-to-T-IR equivalence or unbounded SVA semantics outside this exact reduction.
